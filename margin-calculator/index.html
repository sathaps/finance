How this is calculated

Equity = market value − margin loan. Maintenance requirement = each position's value × its maintenance %. Excess liquidity = equity − requirement. When excess liquidity reaches zero, the broker can sell.

Market-wide drop assumes every position falls by the same percentage: drop = 1 − loan ÷ (market value − requirement).

Single-stock drop holds everything else still: drop = excess liquidity ÷ (position value × (1 − maint. %)).

Real life is worse. IBKR checks margin in real time and generally liquidates without a margin call or grace period. Brokers can raise maintenance rates during volatility or on concentrated positions, and options, short positions and currency balances change the math. Treat the result as the best case and keep a buffer well above it.
